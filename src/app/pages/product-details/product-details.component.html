Caring for Your 3D Creation

To preserve the pristine tactile matte finish and structural geometry of your piece:

  • Temperature: Keep away from prolonged intense direct sunlight or hot vehicle interiors (temperatures exceeding 60°C).
  • Dusting: Gently dust using a soft dry microfiber cloth or soft synthetic bristle brush.
  • Washing: If needed, rinse with cool water and mild organic hand soap. Avoid abrasive scrubbers or solvents.
