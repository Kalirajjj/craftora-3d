Our Mission

Founded in India, Craftora 3D is a modern digital fabrication studio dedicated to turning digital geometries into heirloom-grade physical objects. We believe 3D printing should move beyond plastic prototypes into durable, tactile home accents and meaningful personalized gifts.

Uncompromising Quality

We utilize industrial-grade high-speed enclosed printers with high layer adhesion and specialized custom profiles. Our materials comprise recyclable matte PLAs, engineering PETG, and composite wood polymers that deliver velvety tactile finishes without visible seams.

Eco-Responsible

We prioritize corn-starch derived biodegradable PLA and eliminate single-use plastic in packaging.

Customization

Every piece can be personalized with dates, names, or adapted to bespoke architectural dimensions.

Pan-India Direct

Safe shipping from our Chennai studio directly to doorsteps across India within 3–5 working days.
